Juan Montalvo

I am a retired United States Marine Corps First Sergeant with more than 24 years of military service centered on leadership, training, mentorship, and developing people. I served in operational, logistics, training, and senior leadership roles across the U.S. and overseas — including Platoon Sergeant, Drill Instructor, Company First Sergeant, and Squadron Sergeant Major — and as a Foreign Advisor in the United Arab Emirates supporting the U.S. Department of State. My final assignment was Infantry Company First Sergeant for India Company, 3d Battalion, 3d Marines, in Hawaiʻi.

Leading Marines, Sailors, and international partners across those environments shaped a leadership philosophy built on accountability, teamwork, communication, and taking care of people.

I retired in 2023 and became a Naval Science Instructor with NJROTC at Radford High School, where I help students build leadership, discipline, and character. I hold an A.A. in Supervisory Leadership and a B.A. in Human Resources Development from Hawaii Pacific University, and I'm currently pursuing an Executive MBA at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa's Shidler College of Business.

Outside of work, I stay active through long-distance running, endurance training, hiking, and travel.
