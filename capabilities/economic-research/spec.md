# Economic Research — Specification: Leasing Solar in Hawaiʻi

## 1. Research Question

Will leasing solar save me money over the 25-year contract compared to relying on the electric company, after accounting for lease payments, annual increases, and long-term electricity costs in Hawaiʻi?

## 2. Course Concepts

For my research, I want to focus on time value of money, substitutes, and inflation because these concepts will help me compare the long-term costs of leasing solar with buying electricity from the electric company.

## 3. Planned Sources

- U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA) electricity price data for Hawaiʻi from 2010, 2015, and 2020.
- EIA's Hawaiʻi state page for the 2024 statewide average electricity price (38.00¢ per kWh, which represents all customer types, not just residential customers).
- My personal electric company bills and Sunrun payments.
- My Sunrun lease contract, including the payment schedule and annual increases.
- My Sunrun solar performance review.
- Hawaiʻi Public Utilities Commission reports on net energy metering and solar energy policies.

## 4. Planned Figures

I want to use a line chart to compare the costs over the 25-year lease and show how different increases in electricity prices could affect my savings.

## 5. Success Criteria

I will consider my research successful if I can use my personal bills, lease information, and reliable economic data to determine whether leasing solar is saving me money over the 25-year contract. I also want to understand how increases in electricity prices and lease payments could affect my savings over time and whether leasing makes more financial sense than other options.
